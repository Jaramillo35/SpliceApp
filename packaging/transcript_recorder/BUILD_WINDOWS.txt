Teams Transcript Recorder — Windows build steps
===============================================

What it is
----------
A small tray app that records Microsoft Teams Live Captions into anonymized
markdown transcripts (speakers become Speaker 1..N — participant names are
never written to disk). Same recording logic as the Splice app's Meeting
Transcripts page, for people who want a lightweight recorder without
installing Splice.

Build (one time, on any Windows 10/11 PC with Python 3.11+)
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. C:\TranscriptRecorder)

2. Open a command prompt in that folder and install dependencies:

   pip install -r requirements.txt

3. Build:

   pyinstaller tray_recorder.spec

4. The exe is at:

   dist\TeamsTranscriptRecorder.exe

   Copy it anywhere you like (e.g. your Desktop). You can share this exe
   with coworkers — they do NOT need Python.

Use
---
- Run the exe: a microphone icon appears in the system tray.
- In your Teams meeting turn on Live Captions
  (More > Language and speech > Live captions).
- Recording starts automatically when the captions appear and finishes when
  they close. Transcripts land in a "Transcripts" folder next to the exe.
- Tray menu: Pause/Resume recording, Open Transcripts, Exit.

Notes
-----
- Everything runs on your own PC; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
- Names spoken mid-sentence ("thanks John") cannot be detected and stay in
  the text; the transcript header states this.
- To turn a transcript into meeting minutes, paste it into an LLM with the
  prompt from the Splice app's Meeting Transcripts page.
